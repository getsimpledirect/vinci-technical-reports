# Corrections, Withdrawals, and Narrowings

This report preserves distinct dispositions rather than compressing every change into “retracted.”

- **Negative from the start:** the configured one-epoch recipe missed the frozen efficiency target. It was never a positive result later reversed.
- **Causal interpretation withdrawn:** the apparent two-epoch dose response could not be cleanly attributed to trained weights after the serving-effort control.
- **Correctness claims withdrawn or suspended:** the original executable-code bank failed adversarial qualification.
- **Retained, narrowed:** token counts, termination rates, and cap exhaustion remain interpretable only where they do not depend on the condemned correctness oracle.
- **Contradicted record retained as history:** claim `.011` stated a false-rejection result backwards and was superseded by `.014`.
- **Not measured:** broad safety, product prevalence, Western-base transfer, and production utility.

“Retained, narrowed” is publication vocabulary used by this report. It does not add a new status to the claims registry.
