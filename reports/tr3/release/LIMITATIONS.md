# Limitations

1. **One base-model lineage.** The intervention was studied on Qwen3.8-27B. No Western-base transfer claim follows.
2. **Development-tier evidence.** No external peer review or independent external audit was completed.
3. **Condemned original correctness instrument.** Absolute and relative correctness readings from the original bank are not automatically interpretable; only explicitly oracle-independent token and termination quantities are retained.
4. **Enriched internal tasks.** The banks were selected for mechanisms under study and do not estimate ordinary product incidence.
5. **Two inference seeds for the completed one-epoch evaluation.** This is not an independent replication.
6. **Underpowered matched-effort screen.** Base-at-medium and DPO-at-medium had similar point estimates, but the study could not establish the strict-success non-inferiority margin.
7. **Cap concentration.** Thirty-five of sixty-nine cap hits came from two tasks, preventing a broad model-level termination claim.
8. **Safety scope.** The code bank cannot measure refusal integrity, jailbreak resistance, multilingual use, long-context behaviour, real-world agent authority, or deployment safety.
9. **Probe-population conditionality.** The 154/322, 7/157, and 52/1,862 rates describe deliberately designed probe populations, not candidates emitted by a deployed policy.
10. **Mutation coverage is incomplete.** The first-order AST population did not reach every previously identified defect; 33 distinct gaps is a lower bound, not an exhaustive total.
11. **Protected material remains excluded.** Public evidence bindings permit audit of claims and source identities, but the package does not disclose protected task content.
12. **No DOI or arXiv identifier is embedded.** Those identifiers are assigned by external repositories after upload and belong in repository metadata and the research page.
